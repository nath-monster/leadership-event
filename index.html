Accommodation

Please be advised that this is an overnight event. If you require overnight accommodation, please let us know when completing your RSVP.

Hotel check-in opens from 3:00pm. Guests arriving before check-in will receive information about luggage arrangements before travelling.

Travel

Travel update: A major rail strike has been announced for Tuesday 27 October. If you selected train travel during registration, S & N Pickford Reservations will contact you regarding alternative travel arrangements. If your travel plans have changed since registering, please contact the Partner Event Activation Team.

Our appointed travel partner, S & N Pickford Reservations, will contact you individually following registration to assist with your travel and overnight accommodation arrangements.

Please look out for an email from res@snpickford.co.uk.
If you haven’t heard from S & N Pickford Reservations within three working days of registering, please contact the Partner Event Activation Team.

When completing your RSVP, we’ll ask how you currently expect to travel, so arrangements can be coordinated in advance. If you don’t require travel assistance or overnight accommodation, please let S & N Pickford Reservations know when they contact you.

Further travel, arrival and parking information will be available here as arrangements are confirmed.

What to wear

There is no formal dress code for this event. The programme will involve movement between different spaces throughout the day, so please wear whatever you feel comfortable in. Comfortable footwear is recommended.

Dietary & accessibility

Please tell us about any dietary or accessibility requirements when completing your RSVP, so these can be factored into the event arrangements. If your requirements change after registering, please contact the Partner Event Activation Team.
